with Rong Huang, Ruoqi Han · Corresponding author黄蓉，陶长风（通讯作者），韩若琦

A case study of ChinaScope, a data-driven financial services platform enterprise listed on the Shanghai Data Exchange, under the Ministry of Finance's provisional guidelines. AI implementation and corporate ESG performance: evidence from SMPP adoption

with Min Zheng, Rong Huang · First authorChangfeng Tao, Min Zheng, Rong Huang · 第一作者

Asia-Pacific Journal of Accounting & Economics2025

Using the smart manufacturing pilot program (SMPP) as an exogenous shock to firms' AI investments, we find that AI implementation improves corporate ESG performance. The effect is more pronounced for firms with higher ex-ante information resources and human capital, for firms in regions with stronger environmental enforcement and labor protection, and for non-SOE firms.

以智能制造试点项目（SMPP）作为企业 AI 投资的外生冲击，发现 AI 应用提升了企业 ESG 表现。这一效应在事前信息资源和人力资本水平更高的企业、环境执法和劳动保护更强地区的企业以及非国有企业中更明显。

Recommended citation: 引用格式：Tao, C., Zheng, M., & Huang, R. (2025). AI implementation and corporate ESG performance: evidence from SMPP adoption. Asia-Pacific Journal of Accounting & Economics, 1–30.

Analyst Following and Corporate Cash Holdings: A Perspective Based on Precautionary and Agency Motives分析师关注与企业现金持有——基于预防性动机和代理动机视角

分析师关注与企业现金持有——基于预防性动机和代理动机视角

with Yating Guo, Jianqiao Hong · First author陶长风，郭雅婷，洪剑峭

Management Accounting Studies (管理会计研究)《管理会计研究》2024

Using non-financial A-share listed companies from 2018 to 2023, we find that analyst following is associated with higher corporate cash holdings. Analyst following mitigates financing constraints and reduces precautionary cash reserves, monitors managers by reducing overinvestment and encouraging R&D, and enhances the value of cash holdings.

以 2018—2023 年沪深 A 股非金融类上市公司为样本，发现分析师关注总体上提高了企业现金持有水平。分析师通过缓解融资约束影响预防性动机，通过监督管理层影响代理动机，抑制超额持现带来的过度投资、促进研发投入，并提升了企业价值。

Recommended citation: Tao, C., Guo, Y., & Hong, J. (2024). Analyst following and corporate cash holdings: A perspective based on precautionary and agency motives. Management Accounting Studies, (6), 28–42. (In Chinese)

引用格式：陶长风，郭雅婷，洪剑峭. (2024). 分析师关注与企业现金持有——基于预防性动机和代理动机视角. 管理会计研究, (06), 28–42.

Generative AI and Accounting Comparability

with Rong Huang, Kelvin LiuRong Huang, Kelvin Liu, Changfeng Tao

Working Paper工作论文

We provide evidence that generative AI improves accounting comparability by making financial statements more standardized and more quantitative. Using the release of ChatGPT as a shock and firms' ex-ante workforce exposure to generative AI (GenAI), we find that firms with greater GenAI exposure experience a larger increase in accounting comparability after the release. Using XBRL filings and a novel measure of the similarity of quantitative disclosure in the notes, we show that greater use of standard XBRL concepts shared with industry peers and more similar quantitative disclosure partially mediate the effect. The effect is more pronounced for firms with more accounting judgment, greater operating uncertainty, more generative AI use in financial reporting, and fewer accounting employees.

我们发现，生成式人工智能通过使财务报表更标准化、更定量化，提高了会计信息可比性。以 ChatGPT 发布作为冲击，并结合企业员工事前的生成式人工智能暴露度，暴露度更高的企业在发布后可比性提升更大。基于 XBRL 文件和附注定量披露相似度的新指标，更多使用与行业同行相同的标准 XBRL 概念、定量披露更相似，部分中介了这一效应。该效应在会计判断更多、经营不确定性更高、财务报告中生成式人工智能使用更多、会计人员更少的企业中更明显。

The Regulatory Effectiveness of AI Disclosure: Evidence from Inquiry Letters人工智能信息披露的监管有效性研究——基于问询函的证据

人工智能信息披露的监管有效性研究——基于问询函的证据

Doctoral Dissertation博士论文

This dissertation examines whether AI-related inquiry letters change firms' AI disclosure, and studies their effects on disclosure incentives, capital market pricing, real business decisions, and industry spillovers. I build a letter-level dataset covering more than 20,000 inquiry letters and replies from the Shanghai and Shenzhen Stock Exchanges, construct text-based measures of inquiry intensity, inquiry topics, and reply stance, and identify AI-related letters with a dictionary method.

检验人工智能问询函能否改变企业的人工智能信息披露，并系统考察其对企业披露动机、资本市场定价、实际经营决策与行业溢出的影响。自建覆盖沪深两市两万余封交易所问询函与回函的函件级数据集，构建问询强度、问询主题与回函立场等文本指标；以词典法识别人工智能相关问询函。

Geopolitical Risks and Cost Stickiness

with Rong Huang, Xiao Li, Hong WuRong Huang, Xiao Li, Changfeng Tao, Hong Wu

Working Paper工作论文

Using global supply chain data, we provide evidence that firms with greater exposure to geopolitical risk exhibit stickier SG&A costs through a labor channel, as firms retain costly employees rather than cutting labor during sales declines, particularly when managerial optimism is high and employee protection is strong.

基于全球供应链数据，我们发现地缘政治风险敞口更高的企业，销售及管理费用（SG&A）粘性更强，作用渠道是劳动力：销售下滑时，企业保留成本较高的员工而不是裁员，这在管理层更乐观、员工保护更强时尤为明显。
