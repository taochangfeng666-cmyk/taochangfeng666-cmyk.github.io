I am a PhD candidate in Accounting at the School of Management, Fudan University, advised by Prof. Rong Huang. From September 2025 to August 2026, I was a visiting PhD student at the D'Amore-McKim School of Business, Northeastern University, hosted by Prof. Xiaotao (Kelvin) Liu. My research interests include data assets, artificial intelligence, information disclosure, and cost stickiness. My current work studies how firms account for data assets, how AI shapes corporate disclosure and accounting comparability, and how regulators respond to AI-related disclosure through comment letters.

我是复旦大学管理学院会计学硕博连读博士生，导师为黄蓉教授。2025 年 9 月至 2026 年 8 月，我在美国东北大学达摩尔·麦金商学院联合培养，导师为 Xiaotao (Kelvin) Liu 教授。我的研究兴趣包括数据资产、人工智能、信息披露与成本粘性，目前关注企业如何对数据资产进行会计处理，人工智能如何影响企业披露与会计信息可比性，以及监管机构如何通过交易所问询函回应人工智能信息披露。

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market for faculty positions in Accounting.
